# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

### Added
- Initial standalone Spider Free Online Store 2.0 theme package.
- Basic global color, typography, button, layout, accessibility, SEO, and social settings.
- Basic responsive header, announcement bar, footer, and cart drawer.
- Lightweight product card and basic product / collection / cart / search templates.
- Basic product grid, featured collection, product carousel, and product recommendations.
- Newsletter and Rich Text sections.
- Installation, customization, feature-scope, file-audit, and QA documentation.

### Changed
- Simplified theme layout and section groups.
- Rebuilt the Free product card, collection list, product page, collection page, cart page, and search page.
- Reduced global theme settings to the Free feature scope.
- Replaced premium header, footer, announcement, and cart-drawer implementations with basic Free versions.

### Excluded
- Pro-only sections, snippets, assets, blocks, and templates were excluded from the Free distribution package.
- Original Spider Pro archive was not modified.
